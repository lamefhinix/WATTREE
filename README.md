# WATTREE
It's a small watering system for plants and It will work with solar energy. 

# What my project is......
 WATTREE is a fully automated plant watering system. so... In small gardens, on really sunny days, all the plants don't get enough water for that, and that's why I have created my project WATTREE. How does it work? Whenever the soil is dry, the soil moisture sensor detects it, and a pump runs water through a pipe into the plant and it has and solar panel for energy, and it has a 24-hour battery backup . This system is especially useful when no one is around to water your plants. It automatically takes care of watering them whenever needed, so you don't have to worry about watering them manually.

# how to use it.....
 This is a completely automated system. After setting up, there is no work you have to do. Just a water reserve for it is enough to run it. And I'm also thinking of making a website for it so that you can check all the data. 

# Why you made it? 
The reason for making this was to make gardening easier. It's really often when you forget to water your plants, and the next day, all are dried up. Here, you just have to take care of the water reserve and nothing more.
