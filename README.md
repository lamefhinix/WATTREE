# Wattree 
it's some small watering system for plants, and it works with solar energy. 

# What my project is .....
 Wattree is a fully automated plant watering system. so... In small gardens, on really sunny days, all plants don't get enough water for that, and that's why I have created my project Wattree.
How it works: whenever the soil is dry, the soil moisture sensor detects it, and the pump runs water through a pipe into the plant. It has a solar panel for energy, and it has a 24-hour battery backup.
This system is especially useful when no one is around the garden to water your plants. It automatically takes care of wiring whenever it is needed, so you don't have to worry about watering them manually. 

# How To Use It ...
 This is a completely automated system. After setting it up, there is no work you have to do. Just a water reserve for it is enough to run it. It also has a website to see how much water each plant is getting. 

 #Why I Made It ?
 The reason for making this was to make gardening easier. It's really often that you forget to water your plants, and the next day, all are dried up. Here, you just have to take care of the water reserve and nothing more.
